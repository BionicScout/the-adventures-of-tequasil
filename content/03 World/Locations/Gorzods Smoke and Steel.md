## Description
Gorzod's Smoke and Steel was a shop founded by [[Gorzod Montane]] after moving to [[Knoxopolis]]. The shop came with a few Automaton that could serve has different roles inside the shop. However most of them were broken down. Gorzod was skilled enough to actually get the Automatons up and running again but not with out some "errors" in each of their operations. He set up [[Syntax]] as the Cashier, [[Overflow]] as the stocker, and [[Runtime]] as quality assurance. The store is run by [[Gizmo]], the kobold assistant manager. Gizmo up keeps the automatons in the shop and helps with customers. This allows Gorzod to go work on making more guns and doing business in the city.
