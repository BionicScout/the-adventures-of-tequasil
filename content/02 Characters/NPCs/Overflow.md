## Background
Overflow is one of Automaton [[Gorzod Montane|Gorzod]] set up at [[Gorzods Smoke and Steel|Gorzod's Smoke & Steel]]. Overflow is basic Automaton that stocks the different shelves at the store. He tries his best to optimally layout the stock of the store based on the data of previous attempts. Every other week he attempts to completely rearrange the store to try a new layout to compare against. Gorzod every time proceeds to "argue" with Overflow to put it back, but Overflow has a hard time understanding since he is following his operation. He got his name Overflow because as when he runs out of energy for the day, he starts over stocking the shelves.
