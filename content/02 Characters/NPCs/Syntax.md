## Background
Syntax is one of Automaton [[Gorzod Montane|Gorzod]] set up at [[Gorzods Smoke and Steel|Gorzod's Smoke & Steel]]. Syntax was an experimental Automaton that had the ability to do basic haggling with customers. It strives to get the best mathematical deal possible. But because of its advance ability to bater, other process the Automaton are finnicky. For example, when serving customers, he will sometimes batter using fractions of a copper coin. He also dispense money from a dispensers that look like different types of pistols so that he can fire the change into peoples hands. Sometimes he miscalculates the power needed and will shoot money out the window and call it "Transaction successful". This is bit of turn off to customers coming in, but the gun quality is so high people deal with Syntax, although Syntax has turned away customers before. As it get's later in the day, Syntax's speaking skill deteriorate and he starts to speak in run on sentences, hence his name being Syntax.
