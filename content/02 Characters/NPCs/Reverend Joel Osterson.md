Reverend Joel Osterson is the pastor of [[Mount of Olives Church]] and Head of [[Department of Natural Resources]]. His daughter was kidnapped by the [[The Order of the Crimson Rose|Order of the Crimson Rose]] but was safely returned to him after [[Gorzod Montane|Gorzod]] resecured her. Afterwards, he became a very vocal in support of [[Gorzod Montane|Gorzod]] and his shop.
