## Character Info
### Description
Gorzod Montane is a broad-shouldered, rugged orc gunsmith who operates one of the most respected workshops in Knoxopolis. A master craftsman with a keen eye for detail, he produces reliable, high-quality firearms ranging from sturdy revolvers to powerful long rifles that supply local farms, trading caravans, and town militias. Though his business now thrives, a slight limp in his left leg and the quiet intensity behind his eyes hint at a harder life on the frontier. Customers appreciate his honest work and straightforward manner, but those who know him best understand he is more than a simple shopkeeper. After leading a daring rescue of a government official’s kidnapped daughter from the bloodthirsty [[The Order of the Crimson Rose|Order of the Crimson Rose]], Gorzod’s reputation soared, turning his store into a local success.
