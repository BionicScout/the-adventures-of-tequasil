Gorzod's Smoke and Steel was a shop founded by [[Gorzod Montane]] after moving to [[Knoxopolis]]. The shop came with a few Automaton that could serve has different roles inside the shop. However most of them were broken down. Gorzod was skilled enough to actually get the Automatons up and running again but not with out some "errors" in each of their operations. 

**Syntax (Automaton Cashier)**
[[Syntax ]]was an experimental Automaton that had the ability to do basic haggling with customers. It strives to get the best mathematical deal possible. But because of its advance ability to bater, other process the Automaton are finnicky. For example, when serving customers, he will sometimes batter using fractions of a copper coin. He also dispense money from a dispensers that look like different types of pistols so that he can fire the change into peoples hands. Sometimes he miscalculates the power needed and will shoot money out the window and call it "Transaction successful". This is bit of turn off to customers coming in, but the gun quality is so high people deal with Syntax, although Syntax has turned away customers before. As it get's later in the day, Syntax's speaking skill deteriorate and he starts to speak in run on sentences, hence his name being Syntax.

**Overflow (Automaton Stocker)**
[[Overflow]] is basic Automaton that stocks the different shelves at the store. He tries his best to optimally layout the stock of the store based on the data of previous attempts. Every other week he attempts to completely rearrange the store to try a new layout to compare against. Gorzod every time proceeds to "argue" with Overflow to put it back, but Overflow has a hard time understanding since he is following his operation. He got his name Overflow because as when he runs out of energy for the day, he starts over stocking the shelves. 

**Runtime (Automaton QA)**
[[Runtime]] is a Automaton specialized on doing a quality check on all sold items. He does this by examining each product and run a series of test on them. As the day goes on and he loses energy, he starts to repeat tests. One time Gorzod found Runtime check the spin on a revolver by saying "Test Cycle 457 Complete. Mechanism in perfect order. On to Test Cycle 458". This is where he gets his name run time. 

**Gizmo (Assistant Manager)**
During Gorzod's adventure to find [[Reverend Joel Osterson]]'s daughter, he found [[Gizmo]] as a servant to [[The Order of the Crimson Rose]]. After defeating the leader during the rescue mission, Gizmo lost his "master" and needed to find a new one. Gorzod trying to rescue Gizmo accidently gave him the impression that he was his new master. 

Since then Gizmo has become Gorzod's Assistant. Gizmo was decently skilled with his hands. After some training, Gorzod taught Gizmo how to maintain the Automatons. At the same time Gizmo was taught how to run the basic shop and to run it while Gorzod is not around. Gizmo being around opened up Gorzod's time to be able to do more business deals and smith more weapons. 

While Gorzod is not completely comfortable with being Gizmo's "master", he does see Gizmo as sort of family as he has none left. He provides for Gizmo's housing in a small back room of the shop and food for him to eat.
