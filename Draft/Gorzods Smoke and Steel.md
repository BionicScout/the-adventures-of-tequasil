Gorzod's Smoke and Steel was a shop founded by [[Gorzod Montane]] after moving to [[Knoxopolis]]. The shop came with a few Automaton that could serve has different roles inside the shop. However most of them were broken down. Gorzod was skilled enough to actually get the Automatons up and running again but not with out some "errors" in each of their operations. He set up [[Syntax]] as the Cashier, [[Overflow]] as the stocker, and [[Runtime]] as quality assurance.

**Gizmo (Assistant Manager)**
During Gorzod's adventure to find [[Reverend Joel Osterson]]'s daughter, he found [[Gizmo]] as a servant to [[The Order of the Crimson Rose]]. After defeating the leader during the rescue mission, Gizmo lost his "master" and needed to find a new one. Gorzod trying to rescue Gizmo accidently gave him the impression that he was his new master. 

Since then Gizmo has become Gorzod's Assistant. Gizmo was decently skilled with his hands. After some training, Gorzod taught Gizmo how to maintain the Automatons. At the same time Gizmo was taught how to run the basic shop and to run it while Gorzod is not around. Gizmo being around opened up Gorzod's time to be able to do more business deals and smith more weapons. 

While Gorzod is not completely comfortable with being Gizmo's "master", he does see Gizmo as sort of family as he has none left. He provides for Gizmo's housing in a small back room of the shop and food for him to eat.
