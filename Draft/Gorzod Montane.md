## Character Info
### Description
Gorzod Montane is a broad-shouldered, rugged orc gunsmith who operates one of the most respected workshops in Knoxopolis. A master craftsman with a keen eye for detail, he produces reliable, high-quality firearms ranging from sturdy revolvers to powerful long rifles that supply local farms, trading caravans, and town militias. Though his business now thrives, a slight limp in his left leg and the quiet intensity behind his eyes hint at a harder life on the frontier. Customers appreciate his honest work and straightforward manner, but those who know him best understand he is more than a simple shopkeeper. After leading a daring rescue of a government official’s kidnapped daughter from the bloodthirsty Order of the Crimson Rose, Gorzod’s reputation soared, turning his store into a local success.

### Background
Gorzod Montane (Gore-zod Mon-Tane) grew up in the frontier of Montana. He was born into a small village orcs who has been living in the village for only 8 years. As a kid, he always had a knack for put things together. One example of this was him making a mini firework launcher out materials from his house. His parents took noticed and started to train him to be a smith. Gorzod by the time he was a teenager became the town's gunsmith.

One day Gorzod's villages was ransacked by a horde of goblins under the [[Goblin Chieftain Havre]] ("Have-or") Rattlesnake . The goblins pillaged the small village slaughtering most of the people. During the raid, Gorzod (age 17) was arming his people with guns to fight. However, a goblin was able to shoot Gorzod using one of the fallen orcs' guns. He was hit in the leg and he was no longer able to stand and fight. He then went unconscious. Gorzod then awoke inside the goblin camp. The Chieftain looked over Gorzod with a twisted smile. He then forced Gorzod to make guns for his people as their were no goblin gunsmiths. He forced him by holding some of the other orcs hostage. Gorzod agreed and for a few years armed the Goblins.

During this time, he was able to get his hands on a Bible. As this was one of the few things he could do in his free time he spent. Reading allowed him not only to escape the life he was in but also to find a new one in Jesus. After his coming of faith, Gorzod worked on plans to escape the camp. He secretly was able to build a secret stash of guns while also making the Goblins less reliable guns. At the age of 20, he set his plans into motion. He armed the several of the Orc hostages and set them free. Then together, they were able to break out of the camp.

After escaping, the small groups of orcs returned to their village to only see ruins. While the other orcs who escape decided to stay and rebuild, Gorzod decided to leave. This was in part due to his trying to block out the memories of being imprison and his dead family. He ended up traveling east and then south, finding himself in Knoxopolis after traveling through the [[Forrest of Kyyshack]]. This took about 3 years to do and entered Knoxopolis at the age of 23. 

He was very impressed by the how big [[Knoxopolis]] was and decided it was time to set up business. The first few years of business were very slow, even though Gorzod had good quality weapons. Things changed when one of members of the Knoxopolis government, [[Reverend Joel Osterson]], needed help finding her kidnapped daughter. Feeling a sense of grief from his own experience being kidnapped, he gathered a small crew friends to find the girl. The girl had been taken by [[The Order of the Crimson Rose]], a cult that believes the Great Plains of Texas is a new promised land by God. The new promised land is covered in a deep layer of sin so they must continually sacrifice young maidens to clear sin from the land. Gorzod manage to led the rescue of the girl and partly destroy one of the cults ritual sites. After saving the girl, the government official was deeply grateful and promoted Gorzod's shop. Now Gorzod owns a thriving store in city and has deals with many of the local farms and towns for weapons. 
### Personality
**Traits**
- I am honest and straightforward in my dealings. I take great pride in my craftsmanship and refuse to sell shoddy work, even if it costs me profit.
- I speak little and carry a quiet intensity. I prefer actions over words, but when I do speak, people tend to listen.
**Ideals**
- Redemption through Righteous Work. Everyone deserves a chance to atone for their past and build something better. I craft reliable weapons to help good people protect themselves, and I stand against those who prey on the innocent — just as I was once preyed upon. (Neutral Good)
**Bonds**
- I owe a deep debt to the people of Knoxopolis for giving me a fresh start. The city and its people are now my home, and I will defend them — especially the vulnerable — with everything I have. My faith in Christ also binds me to act with mercy and justice.
**Flaws**
- My past still haunts me. I have trouble forming close relationships and sometimes push people away to avoid reliving the pain of losing my family and village.
- I have a burning hatred for slavers, raiders, and cults. 
- I still carry guilt for the years I spent arming goblins. Because of this, I am sometimes overly cautious about who I sell weapons to, which can cost me business or create enemies.
