During [[Gorzod Montane|Gorzod]]'s time of imprisonment, he became closed to one of the other prisoners. His name was [[Silas the Pilgrim]], a bard from [[New Tanqurray]] who came to spread Christ's word to the people of the [[Forrest of Kyyshack]]. While there, he was captured in a goblin raid. [[Goblin Chieftain Havre]] thought Silas looked funny and was going to torture him for entertainment. However, Silas started to showcase his musical talent to goblins. They were so entertained, they spared him. Soon he became the evening entertainment for the goblins, making him sing and dance for there amusement. 

When later Gorzod arrived to the camp, there was a point where Silas and him were able to talk in private for a few minutes, in which Silas gave Gorzod his bible. Even being imprisoned, Silas used his time to spread Christ message. After a few months, they became good friends and started to plan a breakout of the other prisoners. 

For months before the planned escape, Gorzod spent the little time he got away from the goblins creating a secret stash of guns. When the night came for them to leave, Silas during his normal entertainment of the goblins stopped his singing to drop on his knee. He started to pray over the goblins and for them to find Jesus. When [[Goblin Chieftain Havre]] tried to make him sing, Silas stood up and started singing hymns. During this distraction, Gorzod went to arm the prisoners. 

The plan was for the prisoners to make an escape with the guns and to save Silas in the process. And it was all going according to plan, except [[Goblin Chieftain Havre]] was fed up with Silas' incompliance, so he has his men pin down Silas. As Havre walks over with his a knife to start to filet, Silas continued to pray. Suddenly and Earthquake happen, making the goblins fall down. Silas took the opportunity to run. 

The goblins chased after him. Then Gorzod and the prisoners started to shoot down the goblins. All hell breaks loose as gun fire is traded back and forwards. Gorzod moves to Havre and helps him up. Them and the prisoners start to retreat. However, Silas gets shot in the leg by a shot from Harve. Gorzod tries to keep Silas moving, but he was slowing them down. Silas turns to Gorzod and tells him to go on without him. Gorzod, was reluctant, but he understood they if he stayed, they would both die. Gorzod rushes off with the others. As they were making it into the distance, he see [[Goblin Chieftain Havre]] pull a knife and start stabbing Silas, blood pooling under his body as it went limp.
